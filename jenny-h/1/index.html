Client success handbook

WARMTH, AND EARLY WARNING.

You make Forge members feel a real person is paying attention, and you catch the ones going quiet before they drift off.

Your job

  • Be around in the group channels: react to what people post and join a chat or two.
  • Give a public well-done to one or two people each session.
  • Look after the people on your list that day.
  • Follow up anyone falling behind on their workouts or check-ins. That's a big part of why you're here.
  • Tell Benjamin early if something feels off.

Your list covers Forge members for now. If that changes, it'll change here.

Your week

You're in on Tuesdays and Fridays. Each session, a brief lands in #client-success. Follow-ups go to members in their client inbox channels. You don't need Benjamin to check your messages before they go.

The brief

It's a short stack of cards. Read them in order.

  • Welcome card: your standing job, and a heads-up if the information isn't live.
  • A nice one: someone worth a public well-done.
  • Who needs you: 3 to 5 people. Each card says what's going on, what we're after, their current numbers, and a half-finished opener.
  • Wrap-up: who's held for next time, who's off your plate, and your next session.

The opener is a starter, not a script. Finish it in your own words and never paste it as is. Cards marked "lighter-touch" are gentle hellos, not chases.

Someone lands on your list if they're waiting on you, they've skipped a week of training, they've gone quiet, or an ask has sat too long. If a card looks wrong, tell Benjamin. That's a fault in the brief, not in you.

Keep it chill and natural, like pinging someone you know and want to help. One warm line beats a paragraph. Some members are quiet by nature: notice them, don't nudge them.

Injuries

You can check on people's injuries. Knowing how someone's going is a big part of looking after them.

  • If you think it needs Benjamin, or someone's having trouble training because of it, tell him.
  • Don't promise that Benjamin will look at it or fix it. He'll usually point them to a physio.
  • Don't give advice on it yourself.

Benjamin's side

For these, say "let me get Benjamin on that" and tag him.

  • Training and diet advice, results, programs, the app
  • Money
  • Anyone upset

Who stays, who gets a warning, and who gets moved or removed are always his calls.

How it's measured

  • Are more people sending in their check-ins?
  • Are people actually engaging with you?
  • Is it saving Benjamin time?

We also keep a record of how long people wait to hear back from you, so we can spot it if people are being left waiting. It allows for you working Tuesdays and Fridays.

When you're stuck

Check here first. If it isn't covered, ask Benjamin in #client-success or by DM. Asking always beats guessing.

End of season

Progress photos come up once a season. Chase on day 2 and day 4 after they're due, then stop. If someone owes photos and a check-in, make it one ask. If they've sent some, thank them first, then ask for what's missing. Ask when, not if.

End-of-season check-ins are the one point where members should hear from both of you. You lead, and Benjamin follows.

  • Chase anyone who hasn't filled theirs in.
  • When a member's check-in comes in, they appear on your list as a card that includes what they wrote.
  • Message them in your own words.
  • Benjamin sees the same card and follows up as their coach.
  • If a check-in raises something that's his, tag him rather than answering it.
