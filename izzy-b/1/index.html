Instructions

  1. Pick one breaky, lunch and dinner for the week. Not all combos work together, so some options will be greyed out to keep you on target.
  2. Some ingredients will change in quantity depending on the meal combo. This tool will tell you how much of each ingredient depending on what you pick.
  3. It’ll give you a shopping list for seven days of meals. You’ll end up with two serves of leftovers a week, because you have two weekend meals out. You’ll also need to make sure you follow the recipe portions or you’ll run out of ingredients.
  4. At the end of the week, clear it and choose again to build your next menu.
