For Jared · two weeks away from the gym

Away
from the
gym

One exercise for each body part. Your rings and a chair are all you need.

  • ChestRing push-up
  • BackRing row
  • ShouldersPike push-up
  • BicepsRing curl
  • TricepsRing triceps extension
  • LegsBulgarian split squat

How to use this

  1. 1Run the Forge maintenance plan, 2 sessions a week. It's in Forge under maintenance plans.
  2. 2Swap each exercise for the one here for the same body part.
  3. 3Chase higher reps: 12 to 20. Bodyweight is lighter than the gym, so you get there with more reps.
  4. 4Every set still finishes at the RIR in your plan. More than 20 with reps left? Move up to the harder version.

Two weeks of this holds onto what you've built. Back in the gym, pick up your normal plan where you left off.

Ring
push-up

How to do it · YouTube

Rings at about knee height. Body straight, lower until your hands are by your chest, press back up.

  • Reps12–20
  • Finish each setAt your plan's RIR
  • EasierRings higher, so you're more upright
  • HarderFeet up on a chair, or 3 seconds on the way down

How to do it · YouTube

Ring
triceps
extension

How to do it · YouTube

Lean into the rings with straight arms. Bend only at the elbows to bring your head between your hands, then push back out.

  • Reps12–20
  • Finish each setAt your plan's RIR
  • EasierStand more upright
  • HarderWalk your feet back so you lean further

Legs · 6 of 6

Bulgarian
split
squat

How to do it · YouTube

Back foot up on a chair. Lean forward slightly, lower your back knee towards the floor, drive up through your front foot.

  • Reps12–20
  • Finish each setAt your plan's RIR
  • EasierHold the rings for balance (assisted ring squat)
  • Harder3 seconds down and a 1-second pause at the bottom, or hold a full backpack
