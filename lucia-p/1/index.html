If something hurts, swap it. Don't stop.

You've told me pain is what ended your training before, so this one outranks everything else here.

  • Something hurts during a movement — stop that movement. Not the session.
  • Swap it for one that doesn't hurt. There's a machine version of nearly everything.
  • Tell me in Discord the same day. What hurt, and what you did instead.
  • Then carry on with the rest of the session.

A swapped exercise is a completed session. It isn't a failure, it isn't cheating, and it doesn't need my permission first. Muscles that ache the next day are normal — this is about a joint that says no.

Why no breaking records

This month's a trial run. You're learning the ropes — how the app works, what the exercises feel like, which machines in your gym match the ones in the program. Then the intensity dial goes up, week by week, until you're running it at full tilt on your own.

We've got time to build muscle, get you in shape and get your bone density up. What we can't afford is to scare you, injure you, or put you out of the gym by going too hot too fast.

The machine is the smart choice, not the easy one

The program lists barbells first. For you, the machine version is the better lift, and that's not me going easy on you — a machine takes away the balancing and the positioning, so all your effort goes into the muscle instead of into holding yourself steady.

  • Squat day — use the hack squat or the leg press.
  • Chest press day — use the chest press machine.
  • Deadlift day — film it for me before you load it up.

If a machine you want is taken, don't wait for it and don't skip it. Pick the closest thing that's free, do that, and tell me what you used.

How to film it so I can actually help

Once you've pressed start on a session, tap the name of the exercise. It opens a page that tells you what to focus on, what you should feel, and the angle to film from.

Use that angle. It's the view that lets me see what you're doing well enough to give you something useful back — filmed from the wrong side, I'm guessing.
